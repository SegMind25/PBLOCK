PBLOCK - Family Safety Content Filter

PBLOCK is a parental control and self-accountability tool designed to help families maintain a safe online environment. It blocks access to adult content by modifying DNS settings on your device.

Key Features:
• Blocks 200+ adult websites at the DNS level
• Forces SafeSearch on Google, Bing, and YouTube
• Works on both rooted and non-rooted devices
• Password-protected settings to prevent unauthorized changes
• Survives device reboots for continuous protection
• Two modes: Root DNS modification or VPN-based DNS filtering
• Device protection to prevent uninstallation

How It Works:
PBLOCK operates by intercepting DNS requests and blocking known adult domains. On rooted devices, it modifies the system hosts file directly. On non-rooted devices, it uses a local VPN to filter DNS queries.

Perfect For:
• Parents who want to protect their children from inappropriate content
• Individuals seeking self-accountability tools
• Families looking for an open-source content filtering solution

This app requires device administrator privileges to prevent uninstallation. You can deactivate protection through the app's built-in puzzle challenge system.

Open Source: PBLOCK is free and open source. View the source code on GitHub.
